## Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. For the past two weeks, she has manually recorded daily sales and rough stock levels in a notebook. She requires a data-driven analysis to understand her overall business health, measure week-on-week growth, identify exactly which inventory requires immediate restocking, and determine where to focus her future efforts to maximize returns.

## Top Three Findings
1. **Weekend Dominance:** Weekend sales drive the business, averaging KES 4,900 per day, which is an 82.50% increase over the weekday average of KES 2,685.
2. **Inventory Alerts:** Tomatoes and Sukuma are critically low and require immediate restocking to prevent stockouts. 
3. **Consistent Growth:** The stall demonstrated a solid week-on-week revenue growth of 10.66%, increasing total weekly sales from KES 22,050 to KES 24,400.
